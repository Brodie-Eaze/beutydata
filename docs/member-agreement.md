# Member Agreement (draft — pending legal review)

**Between** the Network operator ("we", "us") and the member business ("Member", "you").

## 1. Eligibility
You warrant that you operate a beauty business with a current and active ABN
registered in Australia, and that the person signing this agreement is
authorised to bind the business.

## 2. Permitted use
The Network is a private information-sharing service for verified members.
You may:
- Log incidents in respect of your own clients only
- Search the Network at the point of booking, having obtained client consent
- Receive aggregate counts of incidents reported by other members

You must not:
- Disclose Network content to non-members
- Use the Network for marketing, debt collection, or harassment
- Log incidents about competitors, employees, or anyone other than a client
- Add free-text characterisations, opinions, or unverified claims

## 3. Accuracy and evidence
For every incident you log you warrant:
- The incident is factually true
- You hold contemporaneous evidence (invoice, booking record, communications)
- You have obtained client consent through the consent clause in your booking T&Cs

## 4. Consent clause distribution
Before logging your first incident you must publish, in your own booking T&Cs,
the consent clause provided in your member dashboard, in substantially the
form supplied. You agree to retain proof of publication.

## 5. Disputes
You will respond to any dispute within 14 days. Failure to respond results in
automatic withdrawal of the incident. Repeated failures to respond may result
in suspension.

## 6. Liability and indemnity
You indemnify us against any claim arising from your entries, including but
not limited to claims in defamation, breach of privacy, or misleading conduct,
to the extent the claim arises from inaccuracy or your failure to comply with
this Agreement.

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate on 30 days' notice. On termination, your entries
remain on the Network until their normal expiry unless withdrawn.

## 8. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [State], Australia.
