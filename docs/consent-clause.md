# Consent clause for member booking T&Cs

Members must include the following clause (in substantially this form) in
their own booking terms and conditions, and must obtain affirmative client
acceptance at the point of booking.

---

> **Information-sharing.** We participate in a private information-sharing
> network used by Australian beauty businesses. Your booking, attendance and
> payment behaviour (for example, unpaid invoices, repeated no-shows, or
> chargebacks) may be shared with other member businesses for the purpose of
> helping them assess booking risk. This is **not** a credit report and does
> **not** affect your credit file. You have the right to access and correct
> information held about you, and to dispute any entry, at
> **[DISPUTE_PORTAL_URL]**. Entries automatically expire 12 months after
> they are recorded.

---

## Implementation notes

- Replace `[DISPUTE_PORTAL_URL]` with the live URL provided to you on signup
- The clause must be presented before the client confirms the booking
- The client's acceptance must be recorded (e.g., checkbox + timestamp)
- Retain proof of acceptance for at least 7 years
